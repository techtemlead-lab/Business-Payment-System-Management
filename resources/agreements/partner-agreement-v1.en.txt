Madina Natural Health Care
Dealer and Sub-dealer Agreement

1. What this agreement is
• This is an agreement between Madina Natural Health Care (here "the company") and you (a dealer or sub-dealer).
• When you press "I have read and agree" in the portal, you accept this agreement.
• When the company publishes a new version, you need to read it and agree again.

2. Joining
• Becoming a dealer or sub-dealer is free. There is no joining fee.
• When you join, you give your national ID (NID) and a photo.
• All the details you give must be true.
• Always carry the company photo ID card while you work.

3. How you earn
• You earn only from real product purchases. Bringing someone in does not earn money by itself.
• The company's rules include these earnings:
  - cashback on your own purchases
  - direct referral commission
  - team bonus on the 2nd and 3rd level
  - area share
  - a monthly salary pool for each division (when the monthly purchase target is met)
  - rank royalty and rank reward
  - car and bike rewards (the company checks and decides these by hand)
• The company sets all rates and targets. They are shown in the portal. What the portal shows is what applies.
• Income is not guaranteed. It depends on real sales and purchases.
• If an order is refunded, the company can take back the commission earned from that order.

4. Orders and payment
• Every order must be paid in full first. Goods are not sent until the full amount is paid.
• Every payment needs a transaction ID (TrxID) and a screenshot of the payment.
• Send money only to the company accounts shown in the portal.
• There is no way to send money from one member to another. The company does not handle such transfers.

5. Selling rules
• Never sell a product above the price printed on it (MRP).
• When you bring in someone new, do not make false income promises. Do not say things like "you will surely earn this much each month".

6. Changes to rates and rules
• The company can change rates and rules.
• Before a change, the company will post a notice in the portal.

7. Complaints and help
• If you have a problem or a complaint, tell us in the portal's "Help" or chat section.
• The company will look at the complaint and resolve it.

8. Your personal data
• Your name, phone, NID, photo and order details are used only for this business.

9. If the rules are broken
• If these rules are broken, the company can close your account for a time or for good.

10. Proof that you agreed
• When you press "I have read and agree", the portal saves the time, your account, your IP address and your device details. This is the proof that you agreed.
