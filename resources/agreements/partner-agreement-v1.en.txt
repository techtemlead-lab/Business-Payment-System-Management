1. The parties and your agreement
• This agreement is between Madina Natural Health Care (here "the company") and the dealer or sub-dealer who accepts it in the portal (here "you").
• When you press "I have read and agree" in the portal, you accept this agreement.
• When the company publishes a new version, you may be asked to read it and agree again.

2. Joining and your account
• You must be at least 18 years old to become a dealer or sub-dealer.
• There is no joining fee to become a dealer or sub-dealer.
• When you join, you give your identity document (national ID or birth certificate) and a photo. All the details you give must be true.
• One mobile number can hold only one account. Only the company's owner can allow an exception, with a written reason.
• Your login, your code and your company photo ID card are for you alone. Do not let anyone else use them.

3. Your code is your refer code
• Your dealer code or sub-dealer code given by the company is your refer code. There is no separate refer code.
• When someone joins through you, this code is entered when their account is opened.

4. How you earn
• You earn only from real product purchases. Bringing someone in does not earn money by itself.
• The company's rules include these earnings:
  - cashback on your own purchases
  - direct referral commission
  - team bonus on the 2nd and 3rd level
  - area share
  - a monthly salary pool for each division (when the monthly purchase target is met)
  - rank royalty and rank reward
  - car and bike rewards (the company checks and decides these by hand)
• The company sets all rates, targets and limits and shows them in the portal. No rate is written in this agreement; what the portal shows is what applies.
• Income is not guaranteed. It depends on real sales and purchases.

5. Money is counted after delivery only
• Every money figure is worked out after goods are delivered, never before. The portal's lists and reports all follow this rule.
• Until goods are delivered, you earn nothing from that order and it is not counted.
• Goods with the courier are neither in stock nor delivered. If they come back, they are added to stock again.

6. Partly delivered, cancelled and returned orders
• If only part of an order is delivered, you earn only on the value of the goods delivered. Goods not delivered go back to stock.
• A cancelled or returned order earns nothing. Such orders and the reason stay visible on your order page.

7. Earnings held, withheld or taken back
• An earning may be held until the company's check is finished. Held money cannot be withdrawn or spent.
• The company may decide, with a written reason, that an order earns no commission. That money goes to the company fund and the order's details stay in the portal.
• If an order is refunded, the company can take back the commission earned from that order. If it cannot be adjusted at once, it is recovered from your later earnings.

8. Orders and payment
• The ways to pay are those shown on the portal's order page: bank, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, cash at the office ("By Office") and your own account's money ("From My Account").
• To use "From My Account", your account balance must be equal to or more than the order's price.
• When you send money by bank or mobile banking, give the transaction ID (TrxID) and a screenshot of the payment.
• Send money only to the company accounts shown in the portal. There is no way to send money from one member to another.
• Goods are not sent until the full amount has been received and checked.

9. Withdrawing money
• You can withdraw only from the money available in your account. A withdrawal cannot be below the minimum shown on the portal's withdrawal page.
• The company checks every request. If a request is refused, the money returns to your account and the reason is shown in the portal.
• When the company has this switched on, you must finish an identity check before your first withdrawal.
• Any tax the law requires is deducted, and the amount deducted is shown in your records.
• Instead of withdrawing, you can place an order for the same amount.

10. Selling and conduct rules
• Never sell a product above the price printed on it (MRP).
• Say only what is printed on the pack or in the company's own papers. Do not say that a product cures a disease.
• When you bring in someone new, do not promise income. Do not say things like "you will surely earn this much each month", and do not take money by promising a job.
• Keep customers' names, phone numbers and health details private.

11. Changes to rates and rules
• The company can change rates and rules. Before a change, the company will post a notice in the portal.

12. Complaints and help
• If you have a problem or a complaint, tell us in the portal's "Help" or chat section. The company will look at it and resolve it.

13. Your personal data
• Your name, phone, identity document, photo and order details are used only for this business.

14. If the rules are broken
• If this agreement or the portal's rules are broken, the company can close your account for a time or for good.
