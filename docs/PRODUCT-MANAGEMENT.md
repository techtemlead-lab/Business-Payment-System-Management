# Product registry and media checkpoint

Historical checkpoint: use [the current requirements-to-code review](COMMISSION-AND-NETWORK-SYSTEM.md#requirements-to-code-review---17-october-2026) for the latest scope comparison and outstanding acceptance items.

14 October 2026, Asia/Dhaka. Client PDF product registry/stock requirements (Q-P03, Q-A05, Q-A06) plus the user's explicit addition: each product can use a YouTube video or a company-uploaded video. Company access remains separate from the storefront.

Company workflow: Product collection → Add product or Product details & media. Deep links are `/company/products/create` and `/company/products/{id}`. Staff can maintain name/SKU/category/unit/description, publication and featured placement, a primary photo, video source, supported prices and low-stock threshold according to permissions. Category suggestions come from existing products; typing a new category creates that grouping through the product. A dedicated hierarchical category editor is still a later module.

Video choices: No video, YouTube Link, Upload Video. One video source is active for a product. A YouTube watch/share/Shorts/embed URL is reduced to a validated eleven-character video ID; arbitrary HTML and external iframe origins are not stored. The generated player uses the [official embed pattern](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters) and [YouTube privacy-enhanced domain](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en). No autoplay or server-side download of that URL occurs. Embed availability depends on the chosen video's owner/settings; browser QA checks embed construction without relying on external playback.

Uploaded videos: MP4/WebM, up to100 MB, served from controlled product routes with native playback and byte-range seeking. Photos: JPEG/PNG/WebP, up to5 MB and4096×4096. Files use random server filenames outside the public webroot. Active product media is public; hidden media is available only to active staff. Replacing/removing a media source removes the superseded local file. Failed/stale updates remove newly staged files. Upload MIME/size checks are enforced on the server. Local PHP upload/post limits are100/112 MB; production web-server limits/storage/backup must match at deployment. This increment does not transcode videos or produce adaptive streams; use browser-compatible MP4 or WebM codecs.

Access: `catalog` edits metadata/photos/videos; `pricing` (currently Super Admin) edits MRP/dealer/cost prices; `inventory` edits stock-alert thresholds. `cost_read` is limited to Super Admin/Finance. Purchase cost is excluded from public and dealer product data and ordinary editor props. Stock cannot be assigned through product editing: use the audited Inventory workflow. Each change requires a reason and registry-version check. Price changes apply to future requests and do not alter existing order-line snapshots. Purchase cost is an informational registry field; no cost-of-goods journal is automatically posted.

Public catalog/detail/homepage and dealer catalog now use saved photos and approved product records. When active non-demo products exist, local reference cards yield to them. Production homepage does not use reference product fixtures. Public product results are filtered/paginated in PostgreSQL. Real product carts accept only controlled same-origin image paths and survive reload. Hidden products are excluded from public pages/dealer catalog and low-stock overview alerts. Homepage promotions still use reference design fixtures until approved client banners are supplied.
